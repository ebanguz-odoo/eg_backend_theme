EG Backend Theme

A modern, themeable backend for Odoo Community: presets, accent, light/dark, density and an app launcher.

Three styles
Aura, Graphite and Mineral, or the original Odoo look with the theme fully off.
Your colors
Six accents, four surface tints, light, dark or follow the system.
Density
Compact, standard, comfortable or wide rows in every list.
Company or personal
The administrator sets the look and can let each user choose their own.
Apps launcher
Search, arrow keys and Enter to open an app.
No flash
Applied by the server, with a live preview while you choose.

Login page, portal, website and reports are not touched. Installing it next to Discuss also themes the chatter through the optional EG Backend Theme - Chatter module. Not for Odoo Enterprise: with web_enterprise installed it does nothing.
