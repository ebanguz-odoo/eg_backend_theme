Operations dashboard for Odoo Community. One screen with the numbers a
sales and operations team checks every day, each one opening the native list
behind it:

* open quotations (count and amount)
* confirmed orders in the chosen period, compared with the previous one
* deliveries to do and late deliveries (if Inventory is installed)
* your overdue activities and the ones due today
* open CRM opportunities (if CRM is installed)
* confirmed amount for the last six weeks and a feed of recent changes

It respects access rights, record rules and the selected companies. Amounts
are shown in the company currency; other currencies are listed apart and are
never converted. It is styled with the EG Backend Theme tokens and also looks
right when the theme is off.
