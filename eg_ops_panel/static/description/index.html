Features

  • Period switch: this month, last month, all time.
  • Six-week chart of confirmed sales, drawn with plain SVG.
  • Feed of the latest changed orders, transfers and opportunities.
  • Respects access rights, record rules and selected companies.
  • Amounts in your company currency; other currencies listed apart, never converted.
  • Stock, CRM and activity cards appear only when those apps are installed.

Works with the theme, or without it

Styled with the EG Backend Theme tokens, with fallbacks so it looks right with the original Odoo look too.
